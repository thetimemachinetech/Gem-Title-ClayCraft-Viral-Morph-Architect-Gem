## 📖 Project Overview

**ClayCraft Viral Morph Architect** eliminates manual prompt engineering for creators targeting the high-RPM clay transformation and ASMR animation niches. 

By combining psychological visual transformation hooks with step-by-step macro sculpting progression, this tool structures production-ready prompts optimized for modern AI video generators including Runway Gen-3, Luma Dream Machine, Kling AI, Wan, and Google Labs Flow.

### 🌟 Key Features
- **Strict 3-Step Gated Workflow:** Trend pitch & ideation $\rightarrow$ Multi-scene text-to-video prompt generation $\rightarrow$ Zero-spam social SEO metadata.
- **Micro-Scene Prompt Density:** Crafts prompts containing 4–5 fast-paced progressive micro-scenes per clip under 900 characters.
- **Embedded ASMR & Voice Cues:** Seamlessly integrates tactile sound cues (`[Tactile ASMR: ...]`) directly into prompts.
- **High Monetization Alignment:** Formatted for Tier-1 algorithmic retention windows (6:30 PM SLST / 4:30 AM SLST).

## 🚀 Quickstart Guide

1. **Open the Gem:** Click the [Custom Gem URL](https://gemini.google.com/gem/1j9z9JKViflvy0JLYOk2BlHMfyMc5ZPQx?usp=sharing) or copy the instructions from `gem.md` into your own Gemini Gem manager.
2. **Start the Flow:** Send `Generate` to receive 3 tailored viral sculpting concepts.
3. **Select & Generate:** Choose your preferred concept to get the complete multi-scene video prompt suite.
4. **Render:** Copy and paste the generated prompts into your preferred text-to-video generator (Runway, Luma, Kling, or Flow AI).
5. **Publish:** Reply `NEXT` to retrieve clean, copy-paste social media metadata and thumbnail prompts.
